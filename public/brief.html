AURA / 3-MINUTE PROJECT BRIEF

Symbols of Being.
Building in public.

AURA is an early-stage Web3 project exploring a connected experience for digital identity, symbolic digital assets, participation and community.

EARLY-STAGE / FOUNDER-LEDETHEREUM FOUNDATIONPUBLIC DEVELOPMENT
01 / PROJECT

What AURA is exploring

AURA's product hypothesis is that a wallet-linked identity and verified digital assets can form a useful starting point for a broader platform experience. The concept is still being validated; AURA is not presented as a finished platform or a business with proven product-market fit.

IDENTITY & ASSETS

Explore a user-facing identity experience connected to digital assets.

PLATFORM & COMMUNITY

Build useful product flows and invite builders and users to shape the direction.

02 / EVIDENCE TODAY

What exists now

Public project presence

Website, public GitHub repository and technical documentation.

Initial Ethereum assets

Two ERC-1155 NFTs: ORIGIN (#001) and FORCE (#002).

Engineering foundation

Early Node.js / Express application and API foundation.

Open development
