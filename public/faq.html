Short answers for investors, builders, and collectors evaluating AURA.

What is AURA?

An early-stage Web3 company exploring digital identity, symbolic assets, and open participation on Ethereum. The first public layer is the ORIGIN and FORCE NFT foundation.

Is there an AURA token?

No. There is no deployed token contract, supply, price, or sale. Any future economic layer is conditional on real product utility and appropriate review.

What can I verify today?

Two ERC-1155 assets on Ethereum, a public contract, a public GitHub repository, and this website at aurasymbol.com.

Is this an investment offer?

No. The site invites evaluation and conversation. Any investment or fundraising structure requires separate legal and regulatory work.

How can I participate?

Investors and partners can contact us. Builders can inspect the repository and propose contributions. Collectors can review the OpenSea collection.

Where is the company online?

Website: aurasymbol.com · GitHub: AURASymbols/aura-web · X: @AURASymbol · OpenSea: aurasymbol collection.
