01 / PROJECT

Understanding AURA

What is AURA?

AURA is an early-stage Web3 project exploring how digital identity, symbolic digital assets, participation and community could work together in a coherent product ecosystem.

What has AURA completed so far?

AURA has a public website and GitHub repository, an established project identity, and two initial ERC-1155 NFT assets published on Ethereum: ORIGIN (#001) and FORCE (#002). The repository also contains an early Node.js / Express application foundation and technical documentation.

Is the full AURA platform already operational?

No. The complete platform is not presented as deployed. The full identity architecture, production infrastructure and broader product experience remain under development or evaluation. See the public roadmap and milestones in GitHub for status details.

What is the first product milestone?

The current MVP direction is: Connect wallet → Establish Identity → Verify NFT → Show AURA Profile. This is a target flow to validate, not a claim that every step is production-ready.

Why is AURA being built in public?

Public development helps people inspect the code, question assumptions and distinguish completed work from future plans. AURA aims to publish verifiable progress rather than unsupported claims about users, revenue, funding or traction.

02 / DEVELOPERS

For builders and technical reviewers

What technologies does the repository use?

The current foundation includes HTML, CSS and browser JavaScript on the frontend, Node.js and Express on the server, ethers for Ethereum integration, and optional PostgreSQL support. Check package.json and the architecture documentation for the current dependencies.

What can developers work on?

Useful early areas include frontend and accessibility, backend/API design, database modeling, wallet and NFT integration, testing, security review, deployment reliability and product architecture.

How should I propose a contribution?

Start with a GitHub Issue describing the problem, proposed solution, expected behavior and verification steps. For a large change, discuss the design first. Keep pull requests focused and include tests or reproducible checks where practical.

Are paid developer positions available?

AURA is not announcing a standing salary, grant, equity or token compensation program. Any paid engagement must be individually scoped and agreed in writing, including payment, timing, intellectual-property ownership and other terms. Do not assume future token rewards.

Can I review or run the code?

Yes. The source repository is public. Follow its README for local setup. Optional services may require your own environment configuration; never commit secrets, API keys, wallet seed phrases or private keys.

03 / INVESTORS & PARTNERS

Evaluating an early-stage project

What problem is AURA trying to solve?

AURA is testing the hypothesis that digital identity, assets and participation can be brought together in a more coherent experience. Product-market fit and user demand are not yet established and must be validated through research and testing.

Does AURA have revenue or a validated business model?

No revenue or validated business model is being claimed here. Potential platform or service models may be researched after product discovery and must be tested for demand, costs and legal obligations.

Is AURA currently raising investment?

This website does not announce an active securities or token offering, a fundraising round, valuation or investment terms. Interested parties may request an exploratory project discussion. Any future fundraising requires a documented structure and appropriate legal review before solicitation or acceptance of funds.

How could support be used?

If a lawful, documented support arrangement is developed, priorities may include product validation, engineering, security, infrastructure and user research. No approved budget percentages or allocation commitments are being represented at this stage.

Who is operating AURA?

AURA is currently founder-led at an early stage. This page does not claim a staffed executive team, employees, advisors or institutional partners unless they are separately identified and verifiable.

04 / TOKEN & RISK

What is not being promised

Has an AURA token been launched?

No. No AURA token contract, supply, price, distribution schedule or token sale is established. A future economic layer is conditional research, not a confirmed issuance.

What would a future token be used for?

That has not been determined. Any future token model must be justified by real product utility and participation, and must undergo technical, economic, security and legal review before implementation or promotion.

What are the main risks?

Product-market fit is unproven; development may require substantial resources; smart contracts and Web3 integrations carry security risks; NFT and digital-asset markets are volatile; and regulation may affect product design or fundraising. Timelines and priorities may change.

Does this page offer an investment opportunity?

No. This page is informational only. It is not an offer to sell or a solicitation to buy securities, tokens or other financial products, and it does not promise returns. Anyone evaluating AURA should conduct independent technical, commercial, financial and legal diligence.

Project status matters. AURA separates what is established, on-chain, building, under research, planned or not deployed. Roadmaps are directional and are not promises of dates or outcomes. Any future fundraising or token-related activity requires qualified legal review.
