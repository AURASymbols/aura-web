AURA's token model is still being researched. We will not publish a token contract, supply or utility before the design and legal framework are ready.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Utility
before speculation.

The purpose of any future token must come from real product utility and participation — not from an artificial promise of price appreciation.

When the model is mature enough to publish, documentation will explain utility, distribution, risks and governance clearly.

No token sale is being presented on this site. Any future token or fundraising activity will be subject to appropriate legal and regulatory review.
