09 / FAQ

QUESTIONS.
STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

Is AURA a finished platform?+

No. AURA is an early-stage project. The initial NFT layer is published; the wider platform remains in development.

Is an AURA token available?+

No token is being offered or represented as deployed. Token design remains research only.

Where can I verify the NFTs?+

ORIGIN and FORCE are published on Ethereum as ERC-1155 assets. Use the OpenSea item links or inspect the contract on Etherscan.

Can developers contribute?+

Yes. The source repository is public. Review the developer guide and open issues before proposing a contribution.
