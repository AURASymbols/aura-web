# AURA — Builder Perspective

AURA is looking for builders who want more than a short-term task.

The project is early enough that architecture, product decisions and engineering standards can still be shaped by the people who join now.

## We are looking for long-term collaborators

> **We are not looking for someone to simply complete a task. We are looking for people who want to build AURA with us for the long term.**

AURA prefers durable working relationships over one-off implementation work whenever the role and circumstances make that possible.

The ideal contributor can grow with the project as the technical and product scope becomes clearer.

## Why join this early?

At a later-stage company, many important decisions have already been made.

At AURA, many of them have not.

A builder joining now can influence:

- System architecture
- API and data design
- Identity architecture
- Web3 integration strategy
- Product workflows
- Security practices
- Testing and deployment standards
- Future technical priorities

The opportunity is therefore not just to implement a specification. It is to help create and improve the specification.

## How we want to work

We want engineering to be based on evidence and iteration:

1. Understand the problem.
2. Define the smallest useful implementation.
3. Build it cleanly.
4. Test it.
5. Document what was learned.
6. Improve the architecture as evidence grows.

We do not want unnecessary complexity simply because the project is Web3.

## Long-term relationship

AURA's preference is to build a core group of contributors who understand the project deeply over time.

As the project develops, long-term contributors may take on broader responsibilities, technical ownership and leadership within their areas.

Specific compensation, equity, token participation or other arrangements will depend on the project's stage, legal structure, role and future agreements. Nothing is promised before it is formally established.

## What we value

- Long-term thinking
- Ownership
- Honest technical communication
- Security awareness
- Practical execution
- Documentation
- Willingness to challenge assumptions
- Ability to work through uncertainty

You do not need to agree with every existing decision. We want builders who can explain why something should be changed and then help make the better version real.

## Current engineering reality

AURA already has a public engineering repository, documented architecture, an API foundation, an initial database direction and Ethereum integration groundwork.

The core platform is still being built.

That means a contributor can join before the architecture becomes difficult to change.
