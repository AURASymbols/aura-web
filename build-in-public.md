# Build in Public

AURA is intentionally being developed in public at an early stage.

## Why

The goal is to let potential contributors see what exists, what is uncertain, and what is being worked on without relying on inflated claims.

## Current build hub

Repository: https://github.com/AURASymbols/aura-web

## Status language

- **Established** — already created or clearly defined.
- **On-chain** — published to a blockchain and externally verifiable.
- **Building** — actively being implemented.
- **Research** — being evaluated before implementation.
- **Design** — architecture or product decisions are still being formed.
- **Planned** — identified work that has not started or is not complete.
- **Not deployed** — no production implementation exists yet.

AURA will update these labels as the project changes. The purpose is clarity, not hype.
