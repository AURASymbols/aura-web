## 1. What AURA is

AURA is an early-stage Web3 project exploring how digital identity, symbolic digital assets, participation and community could work together in a coherent product ecosystem.

The project is being developed in public. This means documenting what exists, what is being built, what remains uncertain, and why important decisions are made.

AURA is not a finished platform or an established investment offering. The public materials distinguish completed work from concepts and future plans.

## 2. The four areas being explored

- **NFTs and digital assets:** the first symbolic expression of the AURA identity.
- **Platform:** a future product layer connecting identity, assets and participation.
- **Token / economic layer:** a research topic only. No token contract, supply, price or sale is established.
- **Community:** developers, creators, users, partners and other contributors who may help shape the project.

## 3. What exists today

- A public AURA website and GitHub repository.
- AURA #001 — ORIGIN, published on Ethereum as an ERC-1155 asset.
- AURA #002 — FORCE, published on Ethereum as an ERC-1155 asset.
- An early Node.js / Express application and public project documentation.

The existence of these assets and code does not mean the complete AURA platform is deployed. See [Milestones](milestones.md), [Architecture](architecture.md), and [API documentation](api.md) for the current technical status.

## 4. What is next

The near-term priority is to strengthen the engineering foundation and validate one useful product experience before expanding the ecosystem.

The current MVP direction is:

`Connect → Establish Identity → Verify NFT → Show AURA Profile`

This is a development target, not a claim that every step is production-ready.

## 5. Who may be a good fit

- Developers interested in frontend, backend, APIs, databases, Web3 or security.
- Product thinkers who can challenge assumptions and define a practical first use case.
- Designers and creators who can strengthen the identity and user experience.
- Strategic partners and potential supporters interested in reviewing the project at an early stage.
