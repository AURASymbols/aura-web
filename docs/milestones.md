## How to Read These Milestones

**Established** means the work exists or the direction is clearly defined.

**On-chain** means an asset has been published to a blockchain and can be independently verified.

**Building** means active implementation is underway.

**Design / Research** means important decisions are still being evaluated.

**Next / Future / Long term** means the work is a target, not a deployment claim.

**Conditional** means AURA will only proceed if evidence and product utility justify the next layer.

## Investment Perspective

AURA is intentionally positioned between concept and product-market validation.

What already exists provides a foundation to evaluate:

- A defined project identity
- Tangible on-chain assets
- A public product portal
- A documented technical direction
- An emerging backend/API foundation
- A clear staged roadmap

What remains open creates the opportunity for early participation:

- Product definition
- Identity architecture
- Engineering execution
- Community formation
- Web3 integration
- Future economic design

**Early participation is about helping determine what AURA becomes, not buying into a finished system.**
