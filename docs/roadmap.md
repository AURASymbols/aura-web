# AURA Roadmap

AURA is being built in stages. The roadmap is directional, not a promise of dates, returns, or outcomes.

## 01 — FOUNDATION / NOW

Establish the identity, initial NFT layer, public presence, and project direction.

- AURA brand and identity
- Initial NFT assets: ORIGIN and FORCE
- Investor / developer web portal
- Public project documentation
- Early community and builder discovery

**Status: NOW**

## 02 — BUILD / NEXT

Turn the current foundation into a stronger technical base.

- Technical architecture
- Backend foundation
- Database design
- API layer
- Core product flows and UX
- Web3 integration research

**Status: NEXT**

## 03 — CONNECT / FUTURE

Connect identity, digital assets, participation and community into a coherent product experience.

- Identity architecture
- Wallet / account flows
- NFT and on-chain data integration
- Community participation
- Platform experimentation

**Status: FUTURE**

## 04 — ECONOMY / CONDITIONAL

Evaluate an economic layer only when real product utility and participation justify it.

- Token utility research
- Economic model
- Smart contracts
- Distribution research
- Governance research
- Legal / regulatory review

**Status: CONDITIONAL**

No token contract, supply, sale, or price promise is established at this stage.

## 05 — ECOSYSTEM / LONG TERM

Expand the ecosystem only after the underlying product, technology and community demonstrate a reason to scale.

- Creator participation
- Developer ecosystem
- Strategic partnerships
- Community-led initiatives
- Global expansion
- New ecosystem layers

**Status: LONG TERM**

## Principle

**Build in stages. Prove. Learn. Expand.**

Priorities may change as AURA is tested, funded, and built.
