# AURA Investor & Strategic Partner FAQ

This document is an informational project overview for people considering a conversation with AURA. It is not an offer to sell securities, tokens or other financial instruments, and it is not investment advice.

## 1. What is AURA?

AURA is an early-stage Web3 project exploring a connected product ecosystem around digital identity, symbolic digital assets, participation and community.

## 2. What has been completed?

AURA has an established project identity, a public website and GitHub repository, and two initial ERC-1155 NFT assets published on Ethereum: ORIGIN and FORCE. The repository also contains an early application and technical documentation.

These milestones should not be confused with a fully deployed platform or proven commercial business.

## 3. What is still being built?

The core product experience, complete identity architecture, production infrastructure, broader Web3 functionality and community model remain under development or evaluation. The current MVP direction is to connect a wallet, establish an identity, verify an AURA NFT and display an AURA Profile.

## 4. What problem is AURA solving?

AURA is exploring whether identity, digital assets and participation can be presented in a more coherent user experience. This is a product hypothesis that must be validated through user research, prototypes and real usage. AURA does not claim product-market fit.

## 5. How could AURA create revenue?

No validated revenue model is being claimed. Potential models may be researched only after product discovery, such as software/platform services, creator tools, integrations or other services that provide clear user value. Any model must be tested for user demand, costs and legal obligations before being treated as a business forecast.

## 6. Is AURA raising capital now?

This public FAQ does not announce an active securities or token offering, a fundraising round, valuation, investment terms or guaranteed allocation. Interested parties may request an exploratory project discussion. Any future fundraising would require a properly documented structure and legal review before solicitation or acceptance of funds.

## 7. Is an AURA token planned?

A future token or economic layer is a conditional research topic, not an established issuance. No token contract, supply, price, distribution schedule or sale is established. Any decision must follow demonstrated product utility, economic analysis, security review and applicable legal and regulatory review.

## 8. What would support be used for?

If a lawful and documented support arrangement is developed, priorities could include product validation, engineering, security, infrastructure and user research. No percentage allocation or budget is being represented as approved at this stage.

## 9. Who is the team?

AURA is currently founder-led at an early stage. This document does not claim a staffed executive team, employees, advisors or institutional partners unless separately identified and verifiable.

## 10. What are the main risks?

- Product-market fit and user demand are unproven.
- The platform is early and may require substantial engineering.
- Web3 infrastructure and smart contracts introduce technical and security risks.
- NFT market activity and digital-asset prices can be volatile.
- Regulatory treatment may affect product design, fundraising and token plans.
- Development timelines, costs and partnerships may change.

People evaluating AURA should conduct their own technical, commercial, financial and legal diligence.

## 11. How can a potential investor or partner evaluate AURA?

1. Review the [project overview](project-overview.md).
2. Inspect the public [GitHub repository](https://github.com/AURASymbols/aura-web).
3. Read the [roadmap](roadmap.md), [architecture](architecture.md) and [milestones](milestones.md).
4. Verify the existing NFT assets through [OpenSea #001](https://opensea.io/item/ethereum/0xb4a9d1ca2ae56e7491f83cb2b7a4c956fa994593/1) and [OpenSea #002](https://opensea.io/item/ethereum/0xb4a9d1ca2ae56e7491f83cb2b7a4c956fa994593/2).
5. Ask questions and request clarification about any claim that is unclear.

## 12. What is the best next step?

An initial conversation should focus on the project's current evidence, the next product milestone, the resources required to reach it, and the risks that need to be resolved. No one should rely on projected token appreciation or promised returns.
